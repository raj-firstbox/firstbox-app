# Installing Firstbox

You need: a computer with internet, and an email address for your login.

## Mac
1. Download the file ending in **`-Mac-arm64.dmg`** (Macs from 2020 onwards, Apple M1/M2/M3/M4 chip) or **`-Mac-x64.dmg`** (older Intel Macs).
   Not sure? Apple menu  › **About This Mac**: "Chip Apple M…" means arm64, "Processor Intel" means x64.
2. Open the downloaded file and drag **Firstbox** into **Applications**.
3. Open **Applications** and double-click **Firstbox**. The first time, macOS says it can’t check the app. Press **Done** (not "Move to Bin").
4. Open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down to the message about Firstbox and press **Open Anyway**, then **Open Anyway** again and enter your Mac password.
5. From now on Firstbox opens normally. Drag it into your Dock to keep it handy.

## Windows
1. Download the file ending in **`-Windows.exe`**.
2. Double-click it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", press **More info › Run anyway**.
3. Firstbox installs and opens by itself, and adds a desktop shortcut.

## First time you open it
- **Raj (owner):** choose **Create account** first, before anyone else. The first account becomes the owner.
- **Team members:** choose **Create account**, type your name and job (e.g. "Ramesh – store"), email and a password.
  You'll see "Request sent". Raj gives you access from ⚙ › Team & access, and your section opens by itself.

## Where files go
Job card PDFs are saved in **Documents › Firstbox › Job cards › (job card number)**. The app opens the folder after saving.
File › **Open Firstbox folder** opens it any time.

## Good to know
- Everyone sees the same data live. It needs internet to save; without internet a message appears at the bottom.
- Forgot your password? Ask Raj for now.
- To sign out (e.g. on a shared computer), press **Sign out** at the top right.
